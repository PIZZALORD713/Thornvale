## Clean-start checklist

- [ ] The participant has not watched or been briefed on the route.
- [ ] ThornVale local storage or the browser profile is clean.
- [ ] The intended character and assets can load without facilitator intervention.
- [ ] The browser console is open only if it will not distract the participant.
- [ ] The facilitator will record help requests before responding.
- [ ] The facilitator will not explain narrative intent until post-play questions end.

## Post-play questions

Ask these before explaining ThornVale or showing another branch.

1. What kind of place is ThornVale?
2. What did the town want from you?
3. When did something first feel unusual or wrong?
4. What do you think happened with the bell and the Ledger?
5. Why did you make your final choice?
6. What did you expect that choice to change?
7. What would you do next if the game continued?
8. Would you replay or inspect the other choice? Why or why not?
9. What was confusing or frustrating?
10. What moment felt most delightful, memorable, or distinctly ThornVale?
