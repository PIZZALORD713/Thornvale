Start from a clean profile or cleared ThornVale save. Say only: “Please play
until the game clearly ends. Think aloud if you are comfortable.” Stay quiet
unless the player asks for help. Ask the questionnaire before explaining the
game's intent.

## Phase questionnaire

1. What did you think you were supposed to do?
2. Was there any point where you felt stuck or needed help?
3. Which activity or moment felt best? Which felt slowest?
4. What did you think the town was doing for you, or asking from you?
5. When did something first feel unusual or wrong?
6. Why did you make your final choice, and what did you expect it to change?
7. What would you want to do next if the game continued?
8. Would you willingly play another day or try the other choice? Why?

### Phase 1 prompts

Use these only when the general answers do not already cover them:

- What did food and energy do?
- How did you feel about the camp after repairing it?
- Did improving the camp change how the Bell or Ledger moment felt?
- Did the repeated chores feel grounded, satisfying, slow, or tedious?

Keep factual observations separate from interpretation. A reproducible
progression, save-integrity, or runtime failure can trigger immediate technical
work. Ordinary friction and subjective preferences join the phase refinement
backlog and should not become a stop-work order from one session.
